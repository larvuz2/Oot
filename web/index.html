Gamepads work out of the box (Xbox layout: A = A, X = B, LT = Z, RB = R, right stick = C). Remap anything from the emulator's controller menu; touch controls appear on phones.

            


            

                
Saving

                
In-game saves are kept in this browser and written every 30 seconds and when you leave the page. Save states (the floppy icon in the emulator bar) are also kept in the browser.

                
The original game and this remix keep separate save files.

            


            

                
How this works

                
    
                    
  1. The game is compiled from C source in the zeldaret/oot decompilation (this repo is a remix of it).
    2. 
                    
  2. CI builds the modified source and publishes only a small BPS delta against the retail ROM.
    3. 
                    
  3. Your browser decompresses your cartridge dump exactly like the decomp's tooling, applies the delta and boots it in an N64 emulator compiled to WebAssembly.
